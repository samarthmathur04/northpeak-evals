# Northpeak Outdoor Gear: Warranty Policy

## 1. What's covered
- **Northpeak-brand** products have a **2-year warranty** against defects in materials and workmanship, starting from the delivery date.
- **Other brands** we sell follow the **manufacturer's warranty**. We'll point the customer to the manufacturer, but we don't handle those claims.

## 2. What's not covered
- Normal wear and tear (faded colors, worn soles).
- Damage from accidents, misuse, or improper care (e.g. machine-drying a down jacket).
- Items that were modified or repaired by someone else.
- Items bought from **unauthorized resellers** (eBay, etc.).

## 3. How a claim works
1. The customer sends photos of the defect and their order number.
2. Support reviews the claim. Simple claims under **$100** can be approved by an agent.
3. Claims **$100 or more** go to the warranty team (escalate). Decision within **5 business days**.
4. If approved, we **repair** the item first. If it can't be repaired, we **replace** it. If it's no longer sold, we give **store credit** for the original price.
- Warranty claims never get a cash or card refund.

## 4. Lifetime warranty
- **Northpeak Summit Series** products have a **lifetime warranty** for the original owner.
- It does **not transfer** if the item is sold or given away.
