# Northpeak Outdoor Gear: Returns & Refunds Policy

## 1. Return window
- Most items can be returned within **30 days of the delivery date**.
- Items must be unused, with original tags attached.
- **Northpeak Plus members** get a **60-day** return window.
- Items bought between **Nov 15 and Dec 31** can be returned until **Jan 31** of the next year, or within the normal window, whichever is later.

## 2. Items that can't be returned
- **Final sale** items (marked "Final Sale" on the product page).
- **Personalized** items (custom embroidery, engraving).
- **Opened consumables**: fuel canisters, food, sunscreen, bug spray.
- **Used gear** can't be returned, unless it's defective (see the Warranty Policy).

## 3. How refunds work
- Refunds go back to the **original payment method**.
- Card refunds take **5–7 business days** after we receive the return.
- **Gift card** purchases are refunded as a **new gift card**, never as cash.
- Original shipping costs are **not refunded**, unless the item was damaged or we sent the wrong item.
- Return shipping is **free for Plus members**. Everyone else pays a **$7.95 return shipping fee**, taken out of the refund.

## 4. Sale items
- Items bought at **40% off or more** (but not marked Final Sale) can be returned for **store credit only**, not a refund to the card.

## 5. Refund limits
- Support agents can approve refunds up to **$200 per order**.
- Refunds **over $200** must be approved by a manager. The agent should escalate and tell the customer to expect a reply within **2 business days**.
- A customer can get at most **3 refunds in any 90-day period**. After that, all further refund requests must be escalated.

## 6. Exchanges
- Size or color exchanges for the **same item** are free within the return window.
- Exchanges for a **different item** are handled as a return plus a new order.
- Final sale items **can't be exchanged**.

## 7. Damaged or wrong items
- Report damaged or wrong items within **14 days of delivery**, with at least **one photo**.
- These get a full refund **including original shipping**, and no return fee.
- Reports after 14 days are handled under the Warranty Policy instead.
