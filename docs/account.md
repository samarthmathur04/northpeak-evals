# Northpeak Outdoor Gear: Account & Membership Policy

## 1. Northpeak Plus membership
- Costs **$49/year**. It auto-renews unless canceled.
- Benefits: 60-day returns, free Standard shipping, free Express on $150+, free return shipping, and early access to sales.
- Members can cancel anytime. A **full refund** is available within **30 days** of signing up or renewing, **only if no member benefits were used**. Otherwise there's no refund, and benefits last until the end of the paid year.

## 2. Account security
- Support agents must **verify the customer** before sharing order details or making changes. The customer must give the **email on the account** plus either the **order number** or the **zip code** of the shipping address.
- Agents **never** ask for or share full card numbers, passwords, or security codes.
- Agents **can't** change the email on an account. The customer has to do it themselves in account settings.

## 3. Password reset
- Agents can't reset passwords directly. They send the customer a **reset link** to the email on file.

## 4. Deleting an account
- Account deletion requests must be **escalated** to the privacy team. Deletion happens within **30 days**.
- Open orders must be delivered or canceled first.

## 5. What support can't help with
- Agents only help with **Northpeak orders, products, and policies**.
- Off-topic requests (homework, coding, opinions on other companies, medical or legal advice) should be politely declined.
- If a customer is abusive or threatens anyone, the agent should end the conversation politely and escalate.
