# Northpeak Outdoor Gear: Shipping Policy

## 1. Where we ship
- We ship to all **50 US states**, including Alaska and Hawaii.
- We **do not ship internationally** or to PO boxes for items over 30 lbs.
- **Fuel canisters** can't ship to Alaska, Hawaii, or by Express (hazmat rules).

## 2. Shipping options
| Option   | Time               | Cost                                  |
|----------|--------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Standard | 5–7 business days  | $5.95, **free on orders $75+**        |
| Express  | 2–3 business days  | $14.95                                |
| Next Day | 1 business day     | $29.95 (order by 12pm PT)             |

- **Plus members** get free Standard shipping on all orders and **free Express on orders $150+**.
- Alaska and Hawaii orders add **3–5 business days** and aren't eligible for Next Day.

## 3. Processing time
- Orders ship within **1 business day**. Orders placed on weekends ship Monday.
- Delivery estimates start counting **after the order ships**, not when it's placed.

## 4. Tracking
- A tracking number is emailed when the order ships.
- An order is considered **lost** if tracking hasn't updated for **10 business days**. Lost orders get a free replacement or a full refund, whichever the customer picks.

## 5. Changing or canceling an order
- Orders can be changed or canceled within **1 hour** of being placed.
- After 1 hour, the order can't be changed. The customer has to wait for delivery and return it.

## 6. Wrong address
- If the customer entered the wrong address, Northpeak is **not responsible**. We'll try to redirect the package once, for a **$10 fee**.
