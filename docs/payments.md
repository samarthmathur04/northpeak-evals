# Northpeak Outdoor Gear: Payments & Pricing Policy

## 1. Payment methods
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Northpeak gift cards.
- We **don't accept** checks, cash, or crypto.
- Up to **2 payment methods** can be used on one order (e.g. a gift card plus a credit card).

## 2. Price adjustments
- If an item's price drops within **14 days** of purchase, the customer can request the difference back **once per item**.
- **How the 14 days are counted:** the purchase date is **day 1**. The window ends at 11:59 PM PT on **day 14**. Example: bought Nov 1, so a price drop on Nov 14 qualifies and a drop on Nov 15 does not.
- Price adjustments **don't apply** to clearance items, flash sales, or competitors' prices.

## 3. Promo codes
- Only **one promo code** per order.
- Promo codes **can't be applied after the order is placed**.
- Promo codes can't be used on gift cards or Plus memberships.

## 4. Gift cards
- Gift cards **never expire** and can't be exchanged for cash (except where required by state law).
- Lost or stolen gift cards can only be replaced if the customer has the **original receipt** or the card number.

## 5. Sales tax
- Sales tax is charged based on the **shipping address**.
- Support agents **can't remove or refund sales tax**. Tax-exempt organizations must apply through a separate form, which goes to escalation.

## 6. Failed payments
- If a payment fails, the order is held for **24 hours** and then canceled automatically.
